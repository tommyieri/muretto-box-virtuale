Two questions answered by the same data. Where a team stands between top speed and cornering speed — that is, where it has chosen to be slow. And what character a track has between engine and downforce: how much of the lap is spent at full throttle.

Set-up · top speed against cornering

Each dot is a team: to the right the ones quick on the straight, at the top the ones quick in the corners. The diagonal is not a verdict — it is the compromise, and everyone resolves it their own way.

Circuit DNA · engine against downforce

The share of the lap at full throttle is the cleanest signature of a track: the higher it is, the more the engine counts; the lower it is, the more aerodynamic downforce counts.
